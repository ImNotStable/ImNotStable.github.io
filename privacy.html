Jarvis – Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-22

Jarvis is a personal assistant application built and used by one person, its owner, Jeremiah Hughes. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, Jarvis reads the owner's Gmail messages (read-only; it cannot send, delete or change mail) and reads and manages events in the owner's Google Calendar.

How data is used

The data is used only to give the owner summaries, reminders and answers through the owner's private Telegram chat. To produce them, relevant excerpts may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) under its API terms.

Where data is stored

Data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer or private server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API data

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

The owner can delete the local data at any time and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
